RISK REGISTER – CAREERPILOT AI
Risk Probability Impact Mitigation Plan
1. Cold-start & inaccurate match High High Enforce onboarding requirement of 3 baseline skills and use hybrid keyword/vector cosine matching fallbacks.
2. Skill evidence fabrication or broken links Medium High Implement automated repo regex checks, certificate domain whitelisting, and mandatory checkpoint quiz validation.
3. Client-side latency & radar visualizer lag Medium Medium Debounce filter inputs, optimize vector computations with async routines, and cache precomputed job match scores.
4. Authentication & data security vulnerabilities Medium High Use secure token validation, password hashing, input sanitization, and role-based access control (Student / Recruiter).
5. Assessment test fatigue or drop-off Medium Medium Structure assessments into bite-sized 5-question micro-quizzes with real-time auto-save and progress indicators.
