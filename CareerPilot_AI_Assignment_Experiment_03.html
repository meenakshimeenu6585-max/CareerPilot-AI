1. Aim of the Experiment

To design and formulate a comprehensive Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), construct an interactive Gantt Chart schedule to illustrate project timeline tracking, and prepare a Risk Register identifying five major operational/technical risks with probability-impact scoring and mitigation strategies using Microsoft Excel for the CareerPilot AI placement platform.

2. Theoretical Framework & Principles

Proper project planning ensures deterministic milestone completion, optimal human resource utilization, and proactive risk mitigation. This experiment implements three core Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK) pillars:

  • Work Breakdown Structure (WBS): A deliverable-oriented hierarchical decomposition of the total project scope. It adheres to the 100% Rule, ensuring all terminal work packages collectively encompass the full project boundary without overlap or omission.
  • Gantt Chart Scheduling: A graphical horizontal bar chart mapping scheduled tasks against discrete calendar timelines (16 weekly intervals), illustrating concurrency, start-to-finish milestones, and task statuses.
  • Risk Management & Qualitative Scoring: Identification of potential failure modes and assessment via the standard risk exposure formula:
    Risk Score = Probability (1 – 5) × Impact (1 – 5)
    Risk scores range from 1 to 25, categorizing events into Critical (16–25), High (10–15), Medium (5–9), and Low (1–4).

3. Step-by-Step Procedure & Excel Implementation

  1. Workbook Setup: Initiated Microsoft Excel and created structured spreadsheets for WBS Task Decomposition and Risk Register.
  2. Header Schema: Configured mandatory columns: WBS ID, Phase, Module, Work Package, Description, Owner, Start Date, End Date, Duration (Days), Status, and Progress.
  3. Phase Decomposition: Divided the software lifecycle into 6 distinct phases: Initiation, UI/UX Design, Student Platform Core, AI Opportunity Radar, Skills Trust Engine, and Testing/Deployment.
  4. Task & Ownership Allocation: Decomposed phases into 15 discrete work packages and assigned primary ownership across the four group members: Abel Mathew Bose, K.M. Meenakshi, Sen Shaji, and Sidharth K.A.
  5. Duration & Timeline Formulas: Applied dynamic date calculations for project task duration:
    # Excel Duration Formula (Row 6):
    =INT(End_Date - Start_Date + 1) → =H6 - G6 + 1
  6. Gantt Timeline Construction: Built a 16-week matrix (W1 to W16) spanning August 1, 2026 to November 21, 2026. Mapped cell ranges to visualize task execution intervals with color-coded status conditional formatting: Completed (Green), In Progress (Blue), and Planned (Gray).
  7. Risk Register Formulation: Populated 5 critical platform risks, calculating risk severity using:
    # Risk Score Calculation (Row 6):
    =Probability * Impact → =E6 * F6

    # Dynamic Severity Level Logic:
    =IF(G6>=16, "Critical", IF(G6>=10, "High", IF(G6>=5, "Medium", "Low")))
